Social service icons
====================

`x.svg`, `youtube.svg`, `tiktok.svg`, `instagram.svg` and `discord.svg` are from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/)
16.34.0 (`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@16.34.0/icons/<name>.svg`), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md). The only change is
`fill="#ffffff"` on the root element, so the app can tint them to the theme.

`linkedin.svg` is from [Font Awesome Free](https://fontawesome.com) 6.7.2
(`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@fortawesome/fontawesome-free@6.7.2/svgs/brands/linkedin.svg`), by
Fonticons, Inc., licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) (Simple Icons
no longer carries LinkedIn's logo). Changes: `fill="#ffffff"` for tinting, and the view box widened
from `0 0 448 512` to a centred square `-32 0 512 512`; the artwork is untouched.

`github.svg` is from Simple Icons the same way (`fill="#ffffff"` added, CC0 1.0).

`globe.svg` is the `globe` icon from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (lucide-static 0.468.0,
`https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/lucide-static@0.468.0/icons/globe.svg`), licensed
[ISC](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/blob/main/LICENSE). The only change is
`stroke="#ffffff"` instead of `currentColor`, so the app can tint it to the theme. It stands for
ArtCraft's website.

The logos themselves are trademarks of their owners (X Corp., Google LLC, TikTok / ByteDance Ltd.,
Meta Platforms, Inc., Discord Inc. and LinkedIn Corporation). They are used only to link to ArtCraft's own accounts on those services.
